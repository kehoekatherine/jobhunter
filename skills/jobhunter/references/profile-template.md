# Private candidate profile template

Copy this template to a private location. Leave unknown facts blank rather than guessing.

- Items to omit or keep confidential:

## Career history
For each role: employer, accurate title, dates, responsibilities, projects, tools used and evidenced outcomes. Note the source document for facts and whether the candidate has confirmed them.

## Education and qualifications
Include institution, qualification, dates and completion status. Separate ongoing courses from completed credentials.

## Skills and examples
For each relevant skill, record a project or work example and the candidate's actual level of experience.

## Writing preferences
Preferred spelling, tone, length, name and professional contact details to include in private application documents.

## Open questions
Record missing evidence, conflicting dates and claims awaiting confirmation.
